# Raimov System

Stores the developing clinical and operating methodology: diagnostic sequence, interdisciplinary planning, internal referral rules, treatment-plan communication, quality review, handoffs and long-term observation.

The system is in development. Do not describe it as certified, validated or franchisable until modules, reviewers, training, audit criteria and implementation evidence exist.

Medical ownership belongs to Atabek Raimov or the designated clinical reviewer; operational documentation may be prepared by the agency but cannot override clinical approval.
